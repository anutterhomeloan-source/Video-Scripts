# You Don't Need 20% Down

## Hook
Everyone acts like you need 20% down to be a real buyer. If you're sitting there with 3%, thinking you can't compete — I want you to hear this.

## Branding
I'm Jordan, and I close first-time buyer loans with 3% down all the time — I know exactly what makes them work.

## Problem
You feel like a weaker buyer with less down.

## Empathy
That's not on you. Nobody said saving 20% on today's prices was realistic.

## Answer
If you've been saving anything — keeping your credit clean, being careful with your money — that already puts you ahead of where you think you are. Let me tell you about a couple I worked with. They put 3% down. On a $300,000 home. Conventional loan, first-time buyer program. They were still living with their parents, so their timeline was flexible — no rush, no pressure.

VISUAL: text card — "3% down. $300K home. Still a strong offer."

Both had steady income. Proof of funds ready to go. And because we'd already run them fully through underwriting before they made an offer — they were able to shorten their contingencies. That made their offer stronger. Not their down payment.

VISUAL: checklist — "What made it strong: pre-underwritten / proof of funds / shortened contingencies — NOT the down payment" (offer-strength-checklist-card.png)

## Change
So if you've been telling yourself "I can't compete with 3% down" — you might be closer than you think. It's not about the size of your down payment. It's about how ready you are.

## End Result
Buyers stop disqualifying themselves before they even try.
