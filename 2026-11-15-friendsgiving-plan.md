## Part 1

*[Saturday morning. Maya calls, way too excited.]*  
**MAYA:** Jordan! Okay. Don't say no until you hear the whole thing.  
**JORDAN:** That's a scary way to start a phone call. Go ahead.  
**MAYA:** So last night was Friendsgiving. And me, Chloe and Devin had a moment.  
**JORDAN:** A moment.  
**MAYA:** We're all paying like eighteen hundred a month in rent. Separately. And we realized, why are we paying three landlords?  
**JORDAN:** Okay…  
**MAYA:** So we're buying a house. Together. The three of us.  
**JORDAN:** Okay! Honestly? People do that. It can actually work.  
**MAYA:** See! Chloe said you'd say no.  
**JORDAN:** I'm not saying no. I'm saying let's talk about it before anybody signs anything.  
**MAYA:** Too late. We already made a group chat. It's called "The Compound."  
**JORDAN:** Of course it is. So, are all three of you going to be on the loan?  
**MAYA:** Yeah, that way we qualify for more, right?  
**JORDAN:** It can help, yes. But it also means all three of your incomes, all three of your debts, and all three of your credit reports count.  
**MAYA:** Okay, that's fine. We're all pretty responsible.  
**JORDAN:** Love that. And everybody's planning on staying in Atlanta for a while?  
**MAYA:** Oh, totally. I mean… Devin's moving to Denver in March. But other than that.  
**JORDAN:** I'm sorry. Devin's what?  
**MAYA:** It's a job thing! He'll still pay his part. Probably.  
**JORDAN:** Maya. "Probably" is not a word I want anywhere near a thirty-year mortgage.  

## Part 2

*[Same call, a minute later.]*  
**MAYA:** Okay. So is Devin moving a deal breaker?  
**JORDAN:** Not necessarily. But here's what you need to understand. If Devin's on the loan, he's on the loan. Moving to Denver doesn't take his name off it.  
**MAYA:** Wait. So if he stops paying…  
**JORDAN:** Then you and Chloe are still responsible for the whole payment. The lender doesn't split it into thirds.  
**MAYA:** Oh. Oh no.  
**JORDAN:** And it goes the other way too. That mortgage follows Devin when he tries to rent or buy in Denver.  
**MAYA:** So should we just not do it?  
**JORDAN:** No! I actually think buying with friends can work. You just need a plan for the hard stuff before you need it.  
**MAYA:** Like what?  
**JORDAN:** So, who owns what percentage. What happens if somebody wants out. What happens if somebody gets married, or moves, or loses their job.  
**MAYA:** We were mostly talking about paint colors.  
**JORDAN:** I figured. So talk to a real estate attorney and put all of that in writing. It's way less awkward now than it is later.  
**MAYA:** Okay. And Devin?  
**JORDAN:** Maybe he's not on this one. Maybe it's you and Chloe, and we see what the two of you qualify for.  
**MAYA:** That's actually really smart.  
**JORDAN:** Send me both of your information and I'll run it today.  
**MAYA:** Can I at least rename the group chat?  
**JORDAN:** Please do.
