## Hook

Florida is voting this week on whether to slash property taxes for homeowners.  
And meanwhile, in Texas?  
Over a hundred thousand homeowners in just one county may not be claiming a tax break they already qualify for.  
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.  

## Problem

Here's why that matters to your mortgage.  
For most people, property taxes aren't a separate bill.  
They're baked into your monthly payment.  
That's your escrow.  

> VISUAL: NBC Miami screenshot, highlight "$150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028"

So if you're paying more tax than you should…  
you're paying a bigger mortgage payment than you should.  
Every single month.  

## Empathy

And if nobody told you to file for this? You're not alone.  
Nobody tells you at the closing table.  
You sign a hundred pages, you get your keys, and that's it.  

## Answer

In most states, the homestead exemption is not automatic.  
You have to file it yourself. With your county.  
In Dallas County, the Texas Tribune estimates a median-priced home goes from about seven thousand dollars a year in taxes… to about forty-five hundred.  
Just by filing.  
That's roughly two hundred dollars a month back in your payment.  

> VISUAL: Texas Tribune screenshot, overlay "$7,062 → $4,573"

## Stance

So here's my take.  
Before you get excited about a tax cut on a ballot…  
make sure you're claiming the one you already have.  

## Change

Tonight, go to your county tax assessor's website.  
Search your address.  
See if it says "homestead."  
If it doesn't, file.  
Here in Georgia, most counties want it by April 1st.  
And once it's approved, ask your loan servicer to re-run your escrow so your payment catches up.  

> VISUAL: text overlay "1. Search your address 2. Look for 'homestead' 3. File"

## End Result + CTA

Lower taxes. Lower escrow. Lower payment.  
For one form.  
So tell me. When you closed, did anyone tell you to file your homestead exemption?  
Yes or no, in the comments.  
I genuinely want to know how many people are missing this.
