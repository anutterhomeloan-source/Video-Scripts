## Part 1

**KAYLA:** Jordan! We found it. We're writing an offer tonight.  
**JORDAN:** Okay! Tell me everything.  
**KAYLA:** Three bed, two bath, huge backyard. It's listed at three eighty-five.  
**JORDAN:** Love it. How long has it been on the market?  
**KAYLA:** Um… seventy days? Something like that.  
**JORDAN:** Seventy days. Any price drops?  
**KAYLA:** One. It was four hundred.  
**JORDAN:** Okay. And what are you offering?  
**KAYLA:** Full price. Three eighty-five.  
**JORDAN:** Full price.  
**KAYLA:** We don't want to lose it. We lost one in 2022 and I am not doing that again.  
**JORDAN:** I get it. That one hurt. But this isn't 2022.  
**KAYLA:** What do you mean?  
**JORDAN:** In 2022, houses were gone in a weekend. This one's been sitting over two months. The seller already dropped the price once. Did anybody ask the seller for anything?  
**KAYLA:** Like what?  
**JORDAN:** Like help with your closing costs. Or money to buy down your rate.  
**KAYLA:** Wait. You can ask for that?  
**JORDAN:** You can always ask. And right now, a lot of sellers are saying yes.  
**KAYLA:** Okay… so I'll just tell our agent to add that.  
**JORDAN:** Perfect. When are you sending the offer?  
**KAYLA:** …About that.  
**JORDAN:** Kayla.  
**KAYLA:** She sent it twenty minutes ago.  

## Part 2

**KAYLA:** Okay. Our offer went out twenty minutes ago. Full price. Nothing else. Did we just mess this up?  
**JORDAN:** Not yet. Has the seller responded?  
**KAYLA:** No. The deadline's tomorrow at five.  
**JORDAN:** Then nothing's signed. Call your agent. Right now.  
**KAYLA:** And say what? "Never mind"?  
**JORDAN:** No. You're not pulling it. You're revising it. Same price. But now you're asking for a seller credit.  
**KAYLA:** How much do we ask for?  
**JORDAN:** That's a conversation with your agent. But on a house that's been sitting this long? Asking for help is normal.  
**KAYLA:** Won't the seller be mad?  
**JORDAN:** The seller's had zero offers in seventy days. You're the only one at the table.  
**KAYLA:** …Okay. That's fair.  
**JORDAN:** And here's the part people miss. That credit can go toward buying down your rate. So it doesn't just help once at closing. It can lower your payment every month.  
**KAYLA:** So instead of fighting over the price…  
**JORDAN:** You ask for the money that does more for your budget. Let's run both before your agent sends anything.  
**KAYLA:** Okay. I'm calling her.  
*(beat — later)*
**KAYLA:** Jordan! They accepted! Full price, with the credit!  
**JORDAN:** See? Same house. Same price. Better payment.  
**KAYLA:** I almost left that on the table.  
**JORDAN:** You almost did. In this market, the buyer who asks is the buyer who wins.  

## CAPTIONS

Caption 1: Part 1 — The house had sat 70 days with a price drop, and they were about to offer full asking with nothing else. In today's market, a lot of sellers will help with closing costs or a rate buydown if you just ask. Plot twist: the offer was already sent. Would you have offered full price?
Caption 2: Part 2 — The offer was out, but nothing was signed. They revised it to the same price and asked for a seller credit toward buying down the rate. The seller said yes, and the result was a lower payment every month. Did you know you could ask for this?
