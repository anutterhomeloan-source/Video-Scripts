## Part 1

**JORDAN:** Hey Mike! Just calling to check in — we're getting close, underwriting should clear you in a few days.

**MIKE:** Oh, perfect timing actually. Good news on my end too — my trade-in finally died, so I went ahead and got a new car.

**JORDAN:** ...Wait. You financed a new car? Like, an actual auto loan?

**MIKE:** Yeah! Got a great deal on it too. Why, is that a problem?

**JORDAN:** Mike — we are mid-underwriting on your mortgage right now. Any new debt, any new payment, it all counts against you.

**MIKE:** I mean, it's just a car payment. It's like three hundred bucks a month.

**JORDAN:** Three hundred bucks a month is exactly the kind of thing that can push your debt-to-income ratio past the limit. You could go from approved... to denied. Over a car payment.

**MIKE:** Oh no. Jordan, I didn't even think about that. I just needed a car, mine literally wouldn't start anymore.

**JORDAN:** I get it, I do. But this is why I always say — call me before you sign anything. Anything. I need the paperwork on that loan right now, and I need to rerun your numbers immediately.

**MIKE:** Okay, okay, sending it over right now. Am I going to lose the house?

**JORDAN:** I don't know yet. Give me a few hours. Do not finance, charge, or sign anything else until you hear from me. Nothing.

## Part 2

**JORDAN:** Mike, okay. I ran the numbers.

**MIKE:** And?

**JORDAN:** You are right at the edge. Like, right at the edge. Your debt-to-income came back at 49.8%. The program cap is 50%.

**MIKE:** That's... that's insanely close.

**JORDAN:** It is. Two-tenths of a percent. If your car payment had been ten dollars higher, we would not be having this conversation right now.

**MIKE:** Jordan, I am so sorry, I genuinely didn't think a car payment would matter that much.

**JORDAN:** Most people don't. That's the thing — it's not just your mortgage payment that matters anymore. It's every single monthly debt you carry, all added together.

**MIKE:** So I'm okay? We're still closing?

**JORDAN:** We're still closing. But Mike — I need you to hear me on this. From this second until we hand you keys, you do not finance anything. Not a couch, not a TV on a payment plan, nothing. One more surprise like this and it could go the other way.

**MIKE:** Understood. Loud and clear. No more surprises.

**JORDAN:** Good. Because this time we got lucky. Next time, we might not.
