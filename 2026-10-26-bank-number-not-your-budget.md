## Hook

The bank approved you for four hundred thousand.  
So that's your budget, right?  
…Wrong.  
And that one assumption quietly wrecks people's first two years in the house.  
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.  

## Proof

Let's say you're approved for four hundred.  
You find a house at three ninety-five. You're thrilled.  
Six months later, daycare starts.  
The car needs tires.  
And there's nothing left at the end of the month.  

## Answer

Here's what an approval actually looks at.  
Your income. Your debts. Your credit.  
That's about it.  
It doesn't know about daycare.  
Or your gym. Or the trip you take every summer.  
Or that you want to retire someday.  

## Distinction

I'm not saying buy small.  
I'm saying buy on purpose.  

## Change

So do it backward.  
Start with the monthly payment you can actually live with.  
Then we turn that into a price.  
Approved at four hundred? That's roughly thirty-one hundred a month.  
But say your life works best around twenty-six fifty.  
That's closer to a three-forty house.  
That's about four hundred fifty dollars a month back in your pocket.  
VISUAL: Side by side: "Approved: $400K ≈ $3,100/mo" vs "Comfortable: $340K ≈ $2,650/mo" (estimate: 5% down, ~7.3%, taxes/insurance included)

## Stance + Close

Here's the hill I'll die on.  
Approval is the ceiling. Your life sets the floor.  
Save this for the day a lender hands you a number.
