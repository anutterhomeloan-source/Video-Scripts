## Hook

I've turned down loans I could have closed.

Here's why.

## Branding (swap-in)

I'm Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

Sometimes that means telling you no.

## Problem

A couple came in approved for four-fifty.

They wanted to spend every dollar of it.

## Empathy

And I get it.

When someone hands you a number, it feels like permission.

## Answer

But approved isn't the same as comfortable.

That payment would have been about six hundred dollars a month more.

VISUAL: Card, "Approved: $450K · Comfortable: $360K · ~$600/mo difference"

## Change

So I showed them the number that left room for life.

Daycare, car repairs, savings.

## Stance

Here's the hill I'll die on.

A loan you can close isn't always a loan you should.

## End Result

They bought for less.

Six months later, they told me it was the best call they made.

Has a lender ever told you no, or told you to spend less?
