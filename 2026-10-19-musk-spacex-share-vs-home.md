## Hook

Elon Musk says he's giving every kid in America a share of SpaceX.
That's about $146 a kid.
And honestly? That's a cool gift.
But it's not the thing that's going to change your kid's life.
VISUAL: Screenshot of the CNN headline + Musk's X post

If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Problem (the mechanism people miss)

Whatever you think of Elon, this got everybody talking about how to give kids a head start.
But one share isn't wealth.
For most families, wealth isn't in a brokerage account.
It's in the house.

## Receipts

The Federal Reserve just released new numbers this month.
The typical homeowner's net worth? About $459,000.
The typical renter's? About $10,600.
That's more than 40 times the difference.
VISUAL: Screenshot of the Fed report, Table 2, highlight "Owner 458.9 / Renter or other 10.6"

And the typical homeowner's equity alone is around $230,000.
At Friday's price, that's over 1,500 SpaceX shares.
VISUAL: Text call-out "1 share = $146 · Home equity = ~$230,000"

## Empathy + Distinction

Now, homeowners tend to be older and earn more, so it's not all the house.
And if you're renting right now, you didn't do anything wrong.
I'm not saying stocks are bad.
I'm saying the biggest thing most families ever own is the home they live in.

## Change

So if you're a parent renting right now, the head start might start with you.
A lot of people don't realize you can buy with as little as 3 to 3.5% down.
Find out your real number before you assume you can't.

## Stance + Closer

Here's my take.
A share of stock is a nice gift.
A home is a foundation.

## CTA (Discussion question)

So I want to know.
Would you rather your kid get one SpaceX share, or would you rather have help getting into a home?
Tell me in the comments.
