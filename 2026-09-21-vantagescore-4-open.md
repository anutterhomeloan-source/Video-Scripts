## Hook (Validation-First Reframe)

If a lender ever told you "not enough credit history," that doesn't mean you're bad with money.

And this month, something changed.

## Branding

If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Problem

If you mostly pay cash, or you're young, or you're new to credit, that phrase can shut the door.

## Empathy

That's not a character flaw.

Nobody teaches you how to build a file a lender can read.

## Answer

On September ninth, Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac opened up VantageScore 4.0 to all their approved lenders.

Before that, lenders needed special approval.

Now they can choose it on a loan, when it fits.

VISUAL: Source overlay, "FHFA: VantageScore 4.0 open to all approved lenders, Sept 9, 2026"

## Distinction

I'm not saying it fixes a low score.

It doesn't change your history.

It changes how some lenders read it.

## Change

Ask your lender one question: which credit score model are you using on my loan?

VISUAL: Text card, "Ask: Which score model do you use on my loan?"

## Stance

I think this is a quiet change that could matter a lot for the right borrower.

## End Result

A "no" you heard a while back might deserve a second look.

## CTA (Schedule a call)

Schedule a call, link's in my bio, and let's look at your file together.
