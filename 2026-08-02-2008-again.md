## Hook (Misconception Flip)
Everyone's whispering we're one bad headline away from 2008 happening all over again.

…And that panic is talking good buyers out of good decisions.

Because nationally, home prices are actually UP 4.3% since 2022.

Compare that to after 2006, when prices nationally fell 17.3%.

Completely different story.

I'm not saying every market is safe — Austin genuinely corrected hard.

I'm saying the national panic doesn't match the national data.

## Problem
VISUAL: Stat card — "-17.3% (2006-2010) vs. +4.3% (2022-2026)" — source "ResiClub, 8.1.26" (2008-again-stat-card.png)

## Empathy
I get why. The comparisons are scary, and they're everywhere right now.

## Answer
But 2008 was a lending-standards collapse. People got loans they never should've qualified for.

This cycle isn't that. It's regional, and it's about supply, not risky lending.

VISUAL: Market list — "Down: Austin -27.3%, Punta Gorda -24.5% · Up: Rockford +35.1%, Syracuse +33.5%"

Some markets did correct hard — Austin's down over 27%.

But other markets, like Rockford and Syracuse, are up over 30%.

It's not one national story. It's dozens of local ones.

## Change
So before you decide to wait because you're scared of "another 2008" —

look at what YOUR market is actually doing. Not the national headline.
