# The Lock Runs Out Friday

**Type:** Reenactment
**Status:** Scripted
**Date approved:** 2026-09-21
**CTA:** None
**Image asset:** None
**Need:** Certainty (avoid pain) + Contribution

*Illustrative scenario (not a specific real client). Characters: Jordan (loan officer), Taylor (buyer whose closing got pushed past the rate lock expiration). Mechanic: a timing trap — the lock, not the loan, is the problem.*

---

## Part 1

*Jordan's office. Taylor walks in relaxed.*

**TAYLOR:** Hey Jordan, quick update. Closing got pushed to Tuesday.

**JORDAN:** Pushed? By who?

**TAYLOR:** The seller's side. Something with title. It's only a few days.

**JORDAN:** Okay. When does your rate lock expire?

**TAYLOR:** When I close. Isn't that how it works?

**JORDAN:** Not exactly. Your lock has an expiration date.

**TAYLOR:** Since when?

**JORDAN:** Since the day we locked. Forty-five days. It expires this Friday.

**TAYLOR:** This Friday. And closing is Tuesday.

**JORDAN:** Yep.

**TAYLOR:** So what happens Saturday?

**JORDAN:** If we do nothing? Your rate isn't guaranteed anymore.

**TAYLOR:** Isn't guaranteed. So it could change.

**JORDAN:** It could go up or down. But I wouldn't bet on down.

**TAYLOR:** You told me my rate was locked!

**JORDAN:** It is. Through Friday.

**TAYLOR:** Nobody said "through Friday"!

**JORDAN:** It's on your lock confirmation. Page one.

**TAYLOR:** I didn't read page one, Jordan!

**JORDAN:** I know. That's why I'm saying it now, not Monday.

**TAYLOR:** Okay. What's the rate today?

**JORDAN:** Higher.

**TAYLOR:** How much higher?

**JORDAN:** Let me pull it up.

*(pause)*

**TAYLOR:** Actually, don't. I don't want to know.

**JORDAN:** Taylor. You need to know.

*(beat)*

**TAYLOR:** Fine. Is it a lot?

**JORDAN:** It's enough that Friday matters.

## Part 2

**TAYLOR:** Okay. Tell me the damage.

**JORDAN:** Good news first. It's not a disaster. We have options.

**TAYLOR:** I love options.

**JORDAN:** Option one. We extend your lock. That costs a fee.

**TAYLOR:** How much?

**JORDAN:** Depends on the days. Which is why option two matters.

**TAYLOR:** Which is?

**JORDAN:** The delay is on the seller's side. So we ask them to cover it.

**TAYLOR:** Can we do that?

**JORDAN:** We can ask. Your agent puts it in writing today.

**TAYLOR:** And option three?

**JORDAN:** I call title and see if they can pull closing up to Thursday.

**TAYLOR:** Before the lock expires.

**JORDAN:** Exactly.

**TAYLOR:** That's tight.

**JORDAN:** It is. That's why we're calling now.

**TAYLOR:** Why didn't you do that first?

**JORDAN:** I'm doing all three. Right now.

*(Jordan on phone)*

**JORDAN:** Hi, it's Jordan. I need to talk about moving a closing date.

*(cut)*

**TAYLOR:** Did you get it?

**JORDAN:** Title can do Thursday. The seller agreed to cover a short extension as backup.

**TAYLOR:** Backup. I like backup.

**JORDAN:** That's what we're here for.

**TAYLOR:** So my rate holds.

**JORDAN:** Your rate holds.

**TAYLOR:** From now on I'm reading page one.

**JORDAN:** Page one. Page two. All of it.

---

## Captions

Part 1 — His closing got pushed three days, and nobody told him his rate lock doesn't wait. Your lock has an expiration date, and it isn't always your closing date. Do you know yours?

Part 2 — When a delay isn't your fault, it doesn't have to be your cost. There are usually three ways through an expiring lock: move the closing, extend the lock, or ask the other side to cover it. Read your lock confirmation before you need it.
