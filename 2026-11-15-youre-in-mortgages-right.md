## Part 1

*[Thanksgiving table. Jordan has a plate. Aunt Debbie leans in.]*  
**DEBBIE:** Jordan, honey. You're still doing the mortgage thing, right?  
**JORDAN:** I am, yes. Still doing the mortgage thing.  
**DEBBIE:** Okay, good. Because I have some thoughts.  
**JORDAN:** Oh. Okay. Can I at least get some turkey first?  
**DEBBIE:** Rates are seven percent. Seven! That's crazy.  
**JORDAN:** I mean, they're a little over seven right now, yeah.  
**DEBBIE:** You know what my rate was in 1981?  
**JORDAN:** I'm gonna guess… high?  
**DEBBIE:** Eighteen percent. Eighteen. And we were thrilled to get it.  
**JORDAN:** So then seven doesn't sound that crazy, does it?  
**DEBBIE:** Don't get smart with me at the table.  
**JORDAN:** Yes, ma'am.  
**DEBBIE:** Anyway. Your cousin Tyler should just wait until rates go back to three.  
**JORDAN:** Okay, so here's the thing. Three percent was kind of a once-in-a-lifetime thing. If he waits for three, he might be renting until he's sixty.  
**DEBBIE:** Well, then he should do a fifteen-year. That's what we did. Never do a thirty.  
**JORDAN:** A fifteen-year is great if the payment fits. But for a lot of people, the thirty gives them room to breathe. And you can always pay extra on it.  
**DEBBIE:** Hm. Well. Good. Because I told Tyler you'd do his loan.  
**JORDAN:** Oh, I'd love to help him! Tell him to call me next week.  
**DEBBIE:** Next week? He's under contract.  
**JORDAN:** He's what?  
**DEBBIE:** Since Tuesday. He's coming over after pie.  
**JORDAN:** Debbie. Who is doing his loan right now?  
**DEBBIE:** …You are, sweetie. Pass the rolls.  

## Part 2

*[After dinner. Kitchen. Tyler walks in with pie.]*  
**TYLER:** Hey, cuz! Mom said you wanted to talk?  
**JORDAN:** Tyler. Hi. Happy Thanksgiving. So, your mom mentioned you're under contract on a house?  
**TYLER:** Yeah! Big backyard. I'm so pumped.  
**JORDAN:** I love that for you. Who did your pre-approval?  
**TYLER:** My pre-what?  
**JORDAN:** Your pre-approval. The letter that says a lender already checked your income and credit.  
**TYLER:** Oh. No, I was gonna do that part after. Mom said you'd handle it.  
**JORDAN:** Okay. Normally, that part comes first.  
**TYLER:** Is that gonna be a problem?  
**JORDAN:** Not if we move fast. When did you sign?  
**TYLER:** Tuesday.  
**JORDAN:** Okay. So you should still be inside your due diligence period. That's the window where you can still back out and keep your earnest money.  
**TYLER:** Wait. I could lose my earnest money?  
**JORDAN:** If we wait too long and something comes up on the loan side, yes, you could. Which is why we're not waiting.  
**TYLER:** Okay. What do you need?  
**JORDAN:** So I'm going to get your application started tonight. Your last two pay stubs, your W-2s, and your last two bank statements.  
**TYLER:** Tonight? It's Thanksgiving.  
**JORDAN:** I know. And I'd rather do this tonight than call you in two weeks with bad news.  
**TYLER:** Okay. Thank you. Seriously.  
**JORDAN:** Of course. Next time, just call me before you fall in love with a backyard.  
**TYLER:** Deal. You want some pie?  
**JORDAN:** Pass me the pie. And your pay stubs.
