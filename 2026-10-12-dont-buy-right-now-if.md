## Hook

Don't buy a house right now.
At least, not if any of these are you.
And yes, I'm a loan officer telling you this.
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Problem

Rates jumped almost a full point in about a month.
And everybody online is yelling one of two things.
"Buy now before it gets worse."
Or, "The market's broken, don't even try."
VISUAL: Two clashing headlines side by side, both with a red X

## Empathy

If you can afford it, but you're still frozen?
You're not being dramatic.
You just don't want to make a mistake you can't undo.

## Answer

So let me be honest about who should wait.
First.
You barely qualified a few months ago, and now this rate maxes you out.
If the house only works at the very top of your budget, it doesn't work.
VISUAL: Text call-out "Top of budget = doesn't work"
Second.
You're not sure you'll stay three to five years.
Buying costs money. Selling costs money. Short stays usually lose.
Third.
Your income feels shaky.
Like, one of you is about to stop working, and the plan only works on two paychecks.
Fourth.
You'd have nothing left in savings after closing.
The water heater breaks in month two, and now it's on a credit card.
VISUAL: Checklist overlay of all four, checking each one as it's said

## Change

Now go down that list.
If one of those is you, wait. I mean it.
Let's make a plan instead.
But if none of those are you?
Then the rate isn't really what's stopping you.
Fear is.
And fear is something we can work through with real numbers.

## Stance

Here's my take.
I'd rather tell you not to buy than help you buy the wrong house.

## End Result + CTA (Comment "not me")

So go through the list.
If none of them are you, comment "not me."
And let's figure out what's actually holding you back.

## Caption (approved)
Not everyone should buy a house right now, and I'll be the first to say it. But if none of those are you, the rate probably isn't your real problem. Comment "not me" if you made it through the list.
