## Hook (Validation-First Reframe)
Everyone who bought their first home right out of the gate — no waiting, no saving up first — feels like they got the head start.

And if you're sitting there right now with nothing saved yet...

that's not because you did something wrong.

Nobody handed you a manual telling you when you're supposed to be "ready."

If you're not there yet — that might not mean you're behind.

That might mean you're about to do this the right way.

## Branding
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Problem / Empathy (Proof Point)
VISUAL: Text overlay — "you're not behind. you're being careful." (not-behind-being-careful-card.png)

I had a client come to me a while back who had zero savings. Nothing in the bank.

And her biggest fear wasn't the mortgage. It wasn't the process.

It was moving back in with her parents.

She felt like that would mean she failed.

So I told her — don't rush this.

Go move back home for a little while.

That's not failure. That's strategy.

So she did.

She moved back in with her parents, and she started saving — on purpose, with a real plan.

And by the time she was ready to buy? She didn't just barely cover her down payment and closing costs.

She had money left over. Real savings. A real budget.

And she still lives by that budget today.

## Answer
VISUAL: Simple two-line overlay — "Lenders want to see: a down payment. AND a plan."

Waiting until you're actually ready isn't behind.

It's the smart move.

Because lenders want to see reserves. They want to see a plan — not just a down payment.

## Change
So if you're not there yet financially, that's not a reason to feel embarrassed.

That's your sign to start building. On purpose. With an actual plan.

You don't need to have it all figured out today.

You just need a plan to get there.

## End Result
Because the buyers who wait and build first?

They're the ones who close with breathing room — not stress.
