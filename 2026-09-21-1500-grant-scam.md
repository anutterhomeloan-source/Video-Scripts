# The $1,500 Grant

**Type:** Reenactment
**Status:** Scripted
**Date approved:** 2026-09-21
**CTA:** None
**Image asset:** None
**Need:** Certainty (avoid pain) + Contribution

*Illustrative scenario (not a specific real client). Characters: Jordan (loan officer), Kevin (buyer who paid an upfront fee for a "guaranteed" down payment grant). Mechanic: an upfront-fee scam — same scam lane as #12 and #57.*

---

## Part 1

**KEVIN:** Jordan! I found a program that guarantees a twenty-five thousand dollar down payment grant.

**JORDAN:** Guarantees?

**KEVIN:** Guaranteed! No income limits, no waiting.

**JORDAN:** That's a lot of promises.

**KEVIN:** It's a great program!

**JORDAN:** Where did you find it?

**KEVIN:** An ad. Really professional website, testimonials and everything.

**JORDAN:** And what did they ask you to do?

**KEVIN:** Just a small processing fee. Fifteen hundred dollars.

**JORDAN:** Kevin. Did you pay it?

**KEVIN:** ...Maybe.

**JORDAN:** Kevin.

**KEVIN:** I wanted to lock it in before they ran out of spots.

**JORDAN:** Ran out of spots.

**KEVIN:** This morning. Zelle. They said the grant releases in forty-eight hours.

**JORDAN:** Who did you send it to?

**KEVIN:** Their specialist.

**JORDAN:** Do you have a name? A number?

**KEVIN:** A text number. It's not picking up now.

**JORDAN:** Kevin, look at me.

**JORDAN:** Okay. I need you to stay calm.

**KEVIN:** Why? What's wrong?

**JORDAN:** Real down payment assistance doesn't charge an upfront fee to guarantee money.

**KEVIN:** But they had testimonials!

**JORDAN:** Anyone can write testimonials. I could write ten before lunch.

**KEVIN:** Jordan. Is it gone?

**JORDAN:** Call your bank. Right now.

## Part 2

**KEVIN:** *(hangs up)* They opened a case. It depends on whether the payment can be reversed.

**JORDAN:** That's the honest answer. Sooner is better.

**KEVIN:** I feel so stupid.

**JORDAN:** You're not stupid. It's designed to look real.

**KEVIN:** It looked so legitimate.

**JORDAN:** That's the whole point of it.

**KEVIN:** How do I know what's real?

**JORDAN:** Real programs run through state or local housing agencies.

**KEVIN:** Okay.

**JORDAN:** They aren't guaranteed. There are income and property rules, and you apply through an approved lender.

**KEVIN:** Like you.

**JORDAN:** If we're a participating lender. And nobody real asks for a processing fee over Zelle.

**KEVIN:** Or gift cards.

**JORDAN:** Definitely gift cards.

**KEVIN:** So what do I do now?

**JORDAN:** Report it to your state attorney general and the FTC.

**KEVIN:** Will that help?

**JORDAN:** It might stop the next person.

**KEVIN:** Then?

**JORDAN:** Then we look at the real programs. There might be one you qualify for.

**KEVIN:** Real ones exist?

**JORDAN:** They do. They're just not guaranteed in forty-eight hours.

**KEVIN:** Okay. Let's do it the boring way.

**JORDAN:** Boring is how you close.

---

## Captions

Part 1 — He already paid the $1,500 "processing fee" to guarantee a $25K grant. Real down payment assistance doesn't charge an upfront fee for a guaranteed payout. If a program sounds too good to be true and wants money first, stop and ask a lender.

Part 2 — Real programs run through housing agencies, aren't guaranteed, and don't ask for fees over Zelle or gift cards. If you've paid a scam, call your bank right away and report it to your state attorney general and the FTC.
