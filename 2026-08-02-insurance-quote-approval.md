## Hook
Your dream house can fall apart over a form you haven't even seen yet — the insurance quote.

## Branding
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Problem
VISUAL: Stat overlay — "Insurance = 9% of the average mortgage payment nationally" (insurance-9-percent-card.png)

A new report just came out — insurance costs are now eating up 9% of the average monthly mortgage payment. An all-time high.

And in high-risk states, it's double that.

## Empathy
Nobody puts "get an insurance quote" on the house-hunting checklist. Most buyers don't think about it until it's already a problem.

## Answer
Here's something most buyers don't know — insurance companies don't just look at you. They look at the house.

I had a deal where the current owner had filed claims on that home in the past.

That claims history follows the property, not the person.

Combine that with the buyer's own not-so-great insurance history, and the quote came back way higher than anyone expected.

## Change
VISUAL: Text overlay — "Get the insurance quote before you write the offer, not after"

So before you fall in love with a house and write an offer, get an insurance quote on it.

Ask about the property's claims history too — you can request that report before you're locked into a contract.

## End Result
No last-minute scramble. No payment shock right before closing.
