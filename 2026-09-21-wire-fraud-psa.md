# Never Wire Your Down Payment Off an Email

**Status:** Scripted
**Date approved:** 2026-09-21
**CTA:** Save this
**Image asset:** None

PSA — generic warning, not based on a specific client.

## Hook

Your title company emails you wiring instructions.

Same logo, same tone.

And it might not be them.

## Branding

If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Problem

Right before closing, scammers send fake wire instructions that look real.

Once that money moves, it's very hard to get back.

## Empathy

You're stressed, closing is days away, and you want to do everything right.

That's exactly when it works.

## Answer

One rule.

Never wire money based on an email alone.

VISUAL: Mock email card, "Red flags: urgent, new instructions, changed last minute"

## Change

Call the title company.

Use a phone number you already have, from your contract or their official site, not the one in the email.

Confirm the instructions out loud, every time.

If anything feels off, call your bank right away.

VISUAL: Text card, "CALL FIRST"

## Stance

A real title company will never be annoyed that you called.

## End Result

Your down payment lands where it's supposed to.
