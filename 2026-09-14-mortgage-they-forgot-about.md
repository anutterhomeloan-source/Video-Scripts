# The Mortgage They Forgot About

**Type:** Reenactment
**Status:** Scripted
**Date approved:** 2026-09-14
**CTA:** None
**Image asset:** None

*Based on a real client scenario, fictionalized (names and identifying details changed). Characters: Jordan (loan officer), client (buyer), title company (voice) — sellers had a second mortgage they said they'd forgotten about, with insufficient equity (and no outside funds) to close.*

---

## Part 1

**JORDAN:** *(on phone, listening)* Wait — say that again? They have a second mortgage on the home?

**TITLE:** *(voice)* Yep. Looks like it was never paid off or released. They're saying they genuinely forgot it existed.

**JORDAN:** Forgot a second mortgage existed?

**TITLE:** That's what they're telling us. And with what they owe on both loans, there isn't enough equity to cover the payoff and get you to the closing table.

**JORDAN:** Okay. I need to call my buyer.

*(beat, dials client)*

**JORDAN:** Hey — I need to walk you through something title just found, and I want you to hear it from me first.

**CLIENT:** That doesn't sound good.

**JORDAN:** The sellers have a second mortgage on the home. They say they forgot about it. Between both loans, they don't have enough equity to close — and they don't have outside funds to cover the difference either.

**CLIENT:** Wait, so... what does that mean for us?

**JORDAN:** It means right now, they can't close. Not because of anything on our side. Not because of you.

**CLIENT:** I don't understand how you forget a whole mortgage.

**JORDAN:** Honestly? I don't either. But I need you to know — this has nothing to do with your qualification, your credit, anything you did.

## Part 2

**CLIENT:** So what happens now? Do we just lose the house?

**JORDAN:** We gave them time to see if they could come up with the difference. They couldn't. So we're canceling the contract.

**CLIENT:** After everything — the inspection, the appraisal, all of it?

**JORDAN:** I know. I know that's frustrating, and I'm sorry you're hearing this at all.

**CLIENT:** I just feel like we did everything right and it still fell apart.

**JORDAN:** You did do everything right. That's exactly why your earnest money is protected, and why you're not the one scrambling right now. This one's on them, not you.

**CLIENT:** That's... something, I guess.

**JORDAN:** It's more than something. Some buyers don't have that protection built in, and they end up losing money on a deal that was never going to close in the first place. You didn't.

**CLIENT:** So what now?

**JORDAN:** Now we start looking again. You're pre-approved, your numbers are strong, and honestly? The right house is still out there. This one just wasn't it.

**CLIENT:** Okay. Let's find it.
