# The Job Change Nobody Mentioned

- **Type:** Reenactment
- **Status:** Scripted
- **Date approved:** 2026-08-17
- **CTA:** None
- **Image asset:** None
- **Need:** Certainty

*Illustrative scenario (not a specific real client). Borrower character: John — stays at the same company, switches from salary to commission-only, no history of variable income and no eligible cosigner.*

---

## Part 1

*Jordan's office. John, mid-30s, sits down looking pleased with himself.*

**JOHN:** Jordan, hey — good news, actually.

**JORDAN:** Oh yeah? Let's hear it.

**JOHN:** My company came to me this week. They want to switch me from salary to commission.

**JORDAN:** ...Okay. Go on.

**JOHN:** They said the guys on commission are making way more than I am right now. Same company, same role, just paid differently.

**JORDAN:** Same company, though — you're staying a W-2 employee?

**JOHN:** Yeah, still W-2. Nothing else changes. I already told them yes.

**JORDAN:** John. When does that start?

**JOHN:** Next pay period. Why — is that a problem? We close in three weeks.

**JORDAN:** It might be a big problem. Let me explain something about how underwriting looks at income.

**JOHN:** Wait — you're telling me this now?

**JORDAN:** I'm telling you the second I found out. Give me a day to see exactly where we stand.

**JOHN:** Jordan, we already gave notice on our apartment.

*Jordan doesn't answer right away.*

## Part 2

*Two days later.*

**JORDAN:** Okay. I talked to underwriting. I need you to hear all of this.

**JOHN:** That bad?

**JORDAN:** When you're paid commission — or salary, or bonus, anything that isn't a flat guaranteed amount — underwriting needs to see it averaged over two years. Not two weeks. Two years.

**JOHN:** But I haven't even started yet.

**JORDAN:** Exactly. There's zero history. We can't use a single dollar of commission income because there's nothing to average it against.

**JOHN:** Okay, so just use my old salary numbers?

**JORDAN:** That salary doesn't exist anymore as of your next paycheck. I can't qualify you on income that's already gone.

**JOHN:** So what do we do? Can my brother cosign or something?

**JORDAN:** I already looked. His debt-to-income is maxed from his own mortgage. He can't take this on too.

**JOHN:** Jordan, we already gave notice on the apartment. We close in three weeks.

**JORDAN:** I know. And I'm not going to sugarcoat it — with no income history and no cosigner, we can't close this loan right now.

**JOHN:** So all of this... because I said yes to a pay bump.

**JORDAN:** This is exactly why I say it every time — before you change anything about your income, your job, your credit — call me first. Even something that sounds like a raise.
