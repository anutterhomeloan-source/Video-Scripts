## Hook

She'd been told no. Twice.  
And she figured homeownership just wasn't for people like her.  
But that wasn't her fault.  
Nobody had ever shown her the actual path.  
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.  
So let's go behind the sale.  

## Problem

When she called me, she said, "[I'm self-employed. Two banks already said no.]"  
The reason they gave her was [her income on paper looked too low].

## Answer

So we didn't start with "can you qualify."  
We started with "why did they say no."  
And what we found was [the first lender only looked at one year of her income instead of averaging two].  

## Stance

Here's my take.  
A "no" from one lender is information. Not a verdict.  

## Change
