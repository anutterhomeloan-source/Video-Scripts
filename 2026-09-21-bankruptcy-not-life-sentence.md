## Hook (Validation-First Reframe)

If you filed bankruptcy, you probably think you're locked out of owning a home for a decade.

That's usually not true.

## Branding

If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Empathy

I get why it feels that way.

Medical bills, a divorce, a business that didn't make it.

That's not a character flaw.

## Validation

And if you've been paying rent on time and rebuilding your credit since, that counts.

## Proof

Here's the part nobody tells you.

On an FHA loan, it can be as little as two years after a Chapter 7 discharge.

Two years, not ten.

VISUAL: Timeline card, "FHA: ~2 yrs after Chapter 7 discharge · Conventional: usually longer · timelines vary"

## Distinction

I'm not saying every file works.

I'm saying the wait is usually shorter than people assume.

## Change

Find your discharge date and write it down.

Then check your credit report and keep rebuilding.

## Stance

Bankruptcy is a chapter, not the whole story.

## End Result

The question isn't "can I ever buy?"

It's "when, and what do I do until then?"

## CTA (Schedule a call)

If you don't know where to start, start with me.

Schedule a call, link's in my bio, and let's figure out your timeline.
