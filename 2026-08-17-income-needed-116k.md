## Hook (Validation-First Reframe)
Every headline this year is telling you that you need six figures just to buy a house.

And if you don't make that, it feels like the door's already closed.

But that number isn't about you. It's a national average.

Averages don't know your city, your program, or your down payment.

You've been doing the right things — saving, watching the market, being careful with your money.

That's not the problem.

Nationally, Redfin says you need about $116,780 to afford the average home.

But here in Georgia, the number to afford a typical Atlanta-area home is closer to $107,000 — and that's before we even talk about down payment assistance.

So before you count yourself out because of a headline — let's find your actual number.

## Answer
Here's what that headline number doesn't include.

It assumes 20% down. It assumes no assistance. It assumes the "average" home — not the home that's actually right for you.

Change one of those, and your number changes completely.

## Change
That's where a program like Georgia Dream comes in.

It's a state down payment assistance program — and if you qualify, it lowers what you actually need to earn to make the payment work.

VISUAL: Comparison overlay — "National average needed: $116,780" / "Metro Atlanta needed: ~$107,000"

## End Result
You don't need to out-earn a national average.

You need your real number — and it's usually smaller than the headline made you think.
