## Answer
First — compared to a year ago, it's actually a little better. Incomes have been growing faster than home prices, and that's real.
Also — month to month, it's gotten harder since January. The income needed to qualify for a typical home went from about $93,500 in January to over $109,000 by June.

VISUAL: Comparison overlay — "Jan 2026: $93,552 needed" → "June 2026: $109,152 needed" (affordability-tension-card.png)

Both headlines are using a different measuring stick. Neither one is lying to you.

VISUAL: Text overlay — "vs. June 2025: still slightly better year-over-year"

## Change
So stop reacting to whichever headline you saw today.
Ask what it's actually comparing — this month to last month, or this year to last year — before you let it change your plans.
