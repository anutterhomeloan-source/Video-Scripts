## Answer (insider pivot)
But before you go ask for fifty grand, here's what the headline isn't telling you.
A concession is not cash back.
It covers closing costs, prepaids, or buying down your rate.
And your loan caps how much the seller can give.
Conventional is 3, 6, or 9 percent, depending on your down payment.
FHA is 6. VA is 4.
Ask for more than your cap, and the extra just disappears.

VISUAL: Simple table — Conventional 3/6/9% (by down payment) · FHA 6% · VA 4%.

## Change
So know your cap before you write the offer.
Decide where the money goes.
And put the ask in writing. Every time.

## Stance
Here's my take.
If you're writing an offer right now and you're not asking for a concession, you're negotiating against yourself.

## End Result
The seller isn't doing you a favor.
They're paying to get their house sold.
Let them.
