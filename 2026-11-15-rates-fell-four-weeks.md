## Hook

Everybody's been waiting for rates to come down. Well, they've been coming down for a month, and almost nobody noticed.

And if you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, I'm a loan officer here in Atlanta, and I help make mortgages make sense.


## Receipts

So according to Freddie Mac, the average thirty-year rate is now seven point one one percent. That's the fourth week in a row it's gone down, and the lowest it's been in about seven weeks.

> VISUAL: Freddie Mac PMMS screenshot, highlight "7.11%"


## What the headlines aren't covering

But here's what the headlines aren't covering. Buyers haven't come back yet.

According to Realtor.com, about one in five listings had a price cut in October. That's the highest for October since 2022. And fewer homes are going under contract than at this time last year.

> VISUAL: Realtor.com October report screenshot, highlight "20.5% of active listings saw their prices reduced"


## Agency

So right now, you've got rates getting a little better and sellers who are still nervous, both at the same time. And that doesn't last forever. When more buyers come back, sellers stop saying yes to things.

So if you're shopping right now, ask for help with your closing costs, or money toward buying your rate down. And if you've been waiting, at least go get your real numbers so you're ready.


## Stance

So here's my take. The best window usually isn't when rates are at their lowest. It's when rates are getting better and everybody else is still sitting on the sidelines.

And I'm not telling you where rates are going, because nobody knows that. I'm telling you what's happening right now.


## CTA

So I'm curious. Did you even notice rates had come down? Tell me yes or no in the comments, because it tells me a lot about how many people are actually paying attention right now.
