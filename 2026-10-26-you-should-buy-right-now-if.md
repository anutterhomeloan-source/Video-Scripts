## Hook

Everyone's telling you this is the worst time to buy a house.  
And if you've been quietly saving this whole time…  
you've been doing exactly the right thing.  
You didn't miss it. You didn't wait too long.  
You were getting ready.  
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.  

## Setup

A couple weeks ago I told you who should NOT buy a house right now.  
A lot of you commented "not me."  
So this one's for you.  

## Proof

Right now, about one in five homes for sale has had a price cut.  
That's a four-year high.  
And here in Atlanta, the number of homes for sale just hit its highest level in over a decade.  
VISUAL: "21% of listings cut their price — 4-year high" (Realtor.com via AP, Oct 25) + "Atlanta inventory: highest in 10+ years"

## The List

You should buy right now if…  
One. You plan to stay at least five years.  
Two. You'd still have savings left after closing.  
Three. The payment works at today's rate. No "I'll refinance later" required.  
Four. Your income feels steady.  
And five. Homes in your area are sitting and dropping their prices.  
VISUAL: Checklist overlay, ticking off one at a time

## Objection Kill

And if you're thinking, "Yeah, but rates are over seven percent"…  
That's exactly why fewer people are competing with you.  

## Stance + Close

Here's what I believe.  
A bad market for sellers is a good market for prepared buyers.  
If that list sounded like you, comment "that's me."  
And let's talk.
