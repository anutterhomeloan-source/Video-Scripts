# Credit Repair Companies Are Selling You Time You Already Have

**Type:** Talking Head
**Status:** Scripted
**Date approved:** 2026-09-14
**CTA:** Discussion question
**Image asset:** None

---

**Hook**
I'm going to say something that probably won't make me popular with credit repair companies. Most of the time, you don't need them.

**Branding**
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help mortgages make sense.

**Problem**
Here's what usually happens. Someone's getting ready to buy. They're nervous about their score. A credit repair company reaches out, or they go looking for one. And it feels like doing something proactive.

**Empathy**
Seventy-nine to a hundred and fifty dollars a month later, they're paying someone to send dispute letters that, honestly, you can send yourself. For free.

**Answer**
A lot of what these companies do is dispute accounts using pretty standard template letters. Some of it doesn't even fix anything — it just delays accurate information from reporting correctly.

**Change**
So before you pay anyone a monthly fee, call a loan officer. Ask for a free breakdown of exactly what's pulling your score down, and what would actually move the needle. A lot of the time, it's not some mystery that needs a company to solve. It's one or two specific things you can fix yourself, for free, in less time than it took to sign up for the service in the first place.

**End Result**
Same result. Without the monthly fee eating into the down payment you're trying to save.

**CTA**
I want to know — has anyone here actually used a credit repair company and felt it was worth it? Genuinely curious what people's experience was.
