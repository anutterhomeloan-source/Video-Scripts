# The Closed File

**Type:** Reenactment
**Status:** Scripted
**Date approved:** 2026-09-14
**CTA:** None
**Image asset:** None

*Based on a real client scenario, fictionalized (names and identifying details changed). Characters: Jordan (loan officer), client (buyer — shopped his file to another lender mid-process, came back after being denied there).*

---

## Part 1

**JORDAN:** *(phone rings)* Hey, this is Jordan.

**CLIENT:** Hey, Jordan. It's me. This is... this is kind of an awkward call.

**JORDAN:** Okay, what's going on?

**CLIENT:** So, remember when I told you I found a better rate with another lender, and we closed out my file?

**JORDAN:** I remember. You were pretty set on switching.

**CLIENT:** Yeah. Well. Turns out they can't actually approve me.

**JORDAN:** Wait — what do you mean they can't approve you?

**CLIENT:** They ran everything, and now they're saying I don't qualify. Same issue you flagged when we went through my file together.

**JORDAN:** The self-employed income thing?

**CLIENT:** Yeah. That.

**JORDAN:** I told you that wasn't going away just because you switched lenders. That was never a "which lender" problem. That was a "your file" problem.

**CLIENT:** I know. I should've listened. And now... we lost the house.

**JORDAN:** *(sighs)* I'm sorry. I really am.

**CLIENT:** Can I come back? I want to work with you again.

## Part 2

**JORDAN:** I can absolutely bring you back on. But I need something from you first.

**CLIENT:** Anything.

**JORDAN:** You stick with us this time. No shopping around mid-file. If something doesn't feel right, you call me and we talk about it — you don't just go silent and switch.

**CLIENT:** Deal. I promise.

**JORDAN:** And one more thing.

**CLIENT:** What?

**JORDAN:** Your wife needs to hear the truth about what happened to that last house. Not that I dropped the ball. Not that your agent dropped the ball. It wasn't on either of us.

**CLIENT:** I know—

**JORDAN:** I told you, reading through your file, that I didn't think you'd qualify the way you wanted to go about it. You went and shopped it around anyway. That's on you, not on us.

**CLIENT:** *(quiet)* ...Yeah. You're right. I'll tell her.

**JORDAN:** Good. Let's get you re-submitted today. I don't want you losing another house over this.

**CLIENT:** Thank you, Jordan. Seriously.

**JORDAN:** Let's just get it right this time.
