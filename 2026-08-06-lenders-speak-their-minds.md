# If Lenders Spoke Their Minds

- **Type:** Skit (playground-trend spin — new format, one continuous montage)
- **Status:** Scripted
- **Date approved:** 2026-08-06
- **CTA:** None
- **Image asset:** None

*Concept: a mortgage-specific spin on the viral "if adults acted like kids" trend. Jordan plays a lender blurting blunt, unfiltered truths at a string of implied clients/realtors, walking-and-talking to camera. Avoids directly recreating the original creator's literal playground bit — no playground equipment in frame.*

**Location:** Sidewalk, driveway, or park path. Chalk a hopscotch grid onto the pavement beforehand if one isn't naturally there.

**Wardrobe:** Business-casual (blazer/blouse) — the contrast between "professional lender" and skipping down the sidewalk is the sight gag.

**Filming setup:** Solo to camera. Ideal: a second person (or gimbal/stabilizer) walks backward ahead of Jordan, keeping her centered as she walks-and-talks. No second shooter available: stationary phone on a small tripod at each "stop point" — walk into frame, deliver the line, walk off-frame toward the next stop, jump-cut hides the camera move.

**Faking "different people":** Angle body/gaze 20-30° differently for each new "person" so it doesn't read as one continuous address.

---

**1.** *(walking toward camera, spot someone off to the side)*
"Did you get preapproved, or did your dad just say you're good for it?"

**2.** *(same stop, turn slightly, deadpan)*
"I saw your bank statement. That's not a gift — that's a Cash App loan from your cousin."

**3.** *(step closer, lower voice like sharing a secret)*
"Do you know if you finance a car three days before closing, you can lose the whole house?"

**4.** *(beat, matter-of-fact)*
"I said the whole house. Do you have a Venmo?"

**→ WATCH THIS:** *turn, walk a few steps, little skip-hop into the first hopscotch square, keep walking*

**5.** *(arrive at next "person," casual)*
"Should you guys really put that on the store credit card right now? That's gonna cost you the loan."

**6.** *(tilt head, needling)*
"Does that mean you don't actually have the down payment — or did you just round up?"

**→ WATCH THIS:** *skip/hop through two more hopscotch squares, spin slightly on landing*

**7.** *(arrive, faux-concerned)*
"Are you two getting a divorce, or is this just how you talk about escrow?"

**8.** *(brighter, like offering a treat)*
"Do you want to see what your payment looks like if you keep waiting for rates to drop?"

**→ WATCH THIS:** *hop the rest of the hopscotch grid, little victory arm-pump at the end*

**9.** *(arrive, mock-scandalized)*
"Did you just switch jobs? Underwriting's gonna have questions."

**10.** *(perks up, genuinely excited)*
"Do you want to come see my rate sheet? It changes like four times a day."

**→ WATCH THIS:** *jog a few steps away, then turn back like a kid not wanting to leave*

**11.** *(pleading, walking backward away from "them")*
"Don't refinance yet. Don't refinance yet. Can you just wait a little longer? I'm coming."

**12.** *(turns to a new/final "person")*
"Okay, your turn. Guess what this is."

**13.** *(holds up a piece of paper, beat, deadpan reveal)*
"It's a clear-to-close."
