## Part 1

*[Closing day. Jordan at her desk. Ashley FaceTimes.]*  
**JORDAN:** Ashley! Closing day! How are you feeling?  
**ASHLEY:** Jordan, I'm so emotional. Do you know how hard this loan was?  
**JORDAN:** Oh, I know. I was there. The appraisal, the second appraisal, the letter of explanation for the letter of explanation.  
**ASHLEY:** I almost gave up like three times.  
**JORDAN:** But you didn't. And in one hour, you're signing. Where are you right now?  
**ASHLEY:** At the house! Doing the final walkthrough with my agent.  
**JORDAN:** Love that. Show me!  
**ASHLEY:** Okay, here's the living room… Um. Hold on.  
**JORDAN:** What?  
**ASHLEY:** Is carpet supposed to squish?  
**JORDAN:** I'm sorry, what?  
**ASHLEY:** It's like… squishing. Under my shoes.  
**JORDAN:** Okay. Turn the camera down. Ashley, is that water?  
**ASHLEY:** It's wet. Why is it wet? It hasn't even rained.  
**JORDAN:** Okay, don't panic. Can you go check the kitchen?  
**ASHLEY:** Okay, I'm walking… Oh. Oh no. There's water all over the floor.  
**JORDAN:** Okay. Where's it coming from?  
**ASHLEY:** I don't know, my agent's looking for the shutoff… Jordan. Jordan, it's coming from the ceiling.  
**JORDAN:** The ceiling?  
**ASHLEY:** The ceiling is dripping. Why is the ceiling dripping?  
**JORDAN:** Okay. Ashley, I need you to just hear me for one second. Nobody is signing anything in an hour.  

## Part 2

*[An hour later. Jordan on the phone with Ashley.]*  
**ASHLEY:** Okay. They found it. A pipe burst upstairs. They think it's been leaking for days.  
**JORDAN:** I'm so sorry. Are you okay?  
**ASHLEY:** No! I've been waiting for this for months. Can we just close and fix it after?  
**JORDAN:** I know that's what you want to hear. But we can't close today.  
**ASHLEY:** Why not? I still want the house.  
**JORDAN:** Because the house is what's backing your loan. It has to be in the same condition it was in when it appraised. And right now, it's not.  
**ASHLEY:** So whose problem is this?  
**JORDAN:** Until you close, the house is still the seller's. So the damage is on them and their insurance, not you.  
**ASHLEY:** Okay. That's actually a relief.  
**JORDAN:** And honestly? This is exactly why the walkthrough exists. If you had skipped it, you would have signed for a flooded floor and a dripping ceiling.  
**ASHLEY:** And it would have been my problem.  
**JORDAN:** It would have been your problem.  
**ASHLEY:** So what happens now?  
**JORDAN:** So your agent is going to talk to the seller about your options under your contract. Maybe they repair it and we push closing. Or maybe you decide to walk away. Either way, we'll figure out the loan side together.  
**ASHLEY:** I just really wanted to sleep there tonight.  
**JORDAN:** I know. But I'd rather you be upset today than stuck with a flooded house for thirty years.
