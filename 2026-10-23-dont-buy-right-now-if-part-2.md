## Hook

Last week I told you not to buy a house right now.
A lot of you made it through that list.
So here are four more.
And yes, I'm still a loan officer telling you this.

If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.
VISUAL: Text overlay "Don't buy right now if… Part 2"

## Problem

Here's the thing.
Most people who regret buying weren't careless.
They just bought for the wrong reason.
And it didn't feel wrong at the time.

## Empathy

So if one of these hits a little close?
That's okay.
That's the point of the list.

## Answer

First.
You're close to retirement, and the new payment only works on your working paycheck.
If your income is about to drop, don't sign up for a bigger payment right before it does.
VISUAL: Text call-out "Payment > retirement income = ❌"

Second.
You're only buying because everyone around you is.
Your sister bought. Your coworker bought.
That's their timeline. It doesn't have to be yours.

Third.
You're stretching to the very top of your budget…
because you're counting on a refinance later.
Maybe rates come down. Maybe they don't.
Nobody can promise you that. Including me.
If the payment only works after a refinance, it doesn't work.
VISUAL: Text call-out "Hoping isn't a plan"

Fourth.
The payment would cut out everything else you care about.
No trips. No dinners out. Nothing left over.
That's not a home. That's a very expensive cage.
VISUAL: Checklist overlay of all four, checking each one as it's said

## Change

So go down the list again.
If one of these is you, wait.
And let's build a plan for when it's not.
But if none of them are you…
the rate probably isn't what's stopping you.
And that's something we can work through with real numbers.

## Stance

Here's my take.
The right house at the right time beats the perfect rate every time.

## End Result + CTA (Comment "not me")

So, made it through both lists?
Comment "not me."
And let's figure out what's actually holding you back.
