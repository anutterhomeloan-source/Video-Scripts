# Nobody Will Sign It

**Type:** Reenactment
**Status:** Scripted
**Date approved:** 2026-09-14
**CTA:** None
**Image asset:** None

*Based on a real client scenario, fictionalized (names and identifying details changed). Characters: Jordan (loan officer), client (buyer), attorney (voice), listing agent (voice), title company (voice).*

---

## Part 1

**JORDAN:** *(on phone, clearly not the first call)* Hi, this is Jordan Nutter calling again about the outstanding disclosure — has anyone been able to reach the listing agent?

*(hangs up, dials again)*

**JORDAN:** Hey, it's Jordan — following up on the disclosure. Nothing? Okay. Thank you.

*(beat, dials again)*

**JORDAN:** This is the third message I've left this week. We need that disclosure executed by both agents and both parties before we can even think about clear to close.

*(phone rings, Jordan answers)*

**JORDAN:** This is Jordan.

**ATTORNEY:** *(voice)* Hi Jordan — quick question, are we CTC'd yet? We're trying to schedule closing.

**JORDAN:** *(exhales)* No. We are not CTC'd. We are still missing the disclosure I've been asking for, for three weeks now. Both agents, both parties, still unsigned.

**ATTORNEY:** Three weeks?

**JORDAN:** Three weeks. Nobody's picking up. Nobody's answering email. I've called title, I've called both of you, I've called both agents. I am out of numbers to call.

## Part 2

**JORDAN:** *(on phone with client)* Hey — I know this isn't really your job, but has your agent said anything to you? Because she has not returned a single call or email from me in three weeks.

**CLIENT:** No, but let me text her right now.

*(beat, phone rings)*

**JORDAN:** Jordan speaking.

**AGENT:** *(voice, finally)* Hey Jordan, sorry — been slammed. What's up?

**JORDAN:** What's up is this disclosure has been sitting unsigned for three weeks, and I have called you, your broker, both attorneys, and title trying to get it executed. Do you not want to get paid? Do you not want your clients to move into their house and avoid being homeless?

**AGENT:** Whoa — okay. Send it back over, I'll sign it right now.

**JORDAN:** It's been in your inbox since the day it went out.

**AGENT:** I'm looking at it. Signing now.

**JORDAN:** *(to client, relieved)* Okay — she's signing it right now. I'm sending it to the sellers' side next, and the second all four signatures are on it, we are clear to close.

**CLIENT:** Finally.

**JORDAN:** Finally. I'll call you the second it's done.

---

## Captions

Part 1 — One unsigned disclosure held up an entire closing for three weeks, and nobody would answer the phone. This is what it actually looks like when a deal stalls on paperwork nobody's tracking.

Part 2 — Sometimes getting a deal to the closing table means picking up the phone yourself, again and again, until someone finally answers. Here's how it finally got signed.
