# The Detour

- **Type:** Reenactment
- **Status:** Scripted
- **Date approved:** 2026-08-25
- **CTA:** None
- **Image asset:** None
- **Need:** Certainty

*Based on a real client scenario, fictionalized (names and identifying details changed). Characters: Jordan (loan officer), Ryan (buyer — started with Jordan using down payment assistance, left for a mailer offer from another lender, came back two weeks later).*

---

## Part 1

*Jordan's office. Ryan sits across from Jordan, papers already laid out — they'd been working through his file together.*

**JORDAN:** Okay, so with the down payment assistance built in, you're not bringing anything to closing. That's basically locked in on our end.

**RYAN:** That's what I wanted to talk to you about, actually. I got this in the mail.

**JORDAN:** ...A rate flyer?

**RYAN:** Yeah. Says they can beat what we've got. Might even cover more of my closing costs.

**JORDAN:** Ryan, I'm not gonna tell you not to shop around. That's smart. Just — read the fine print on what that flyer's actually promising versus what they can deliver once they pull your file.

**RYAN:** I mean, it's worth a shot, right? Free to check.

**JORDAN:** It is. I just don't want you to lose momentum on your loan while you're checking.

**RYAN:** It won't take long. I'll let you know.

**JORDAN:** Ryan — you already have a plan here that works. Just move fast either way.

**RYAN:** Yeah, yeah. I will.

*He heads out with the flyer still in his hand.*

## Part 2

*Two weeks later. Ryan sits back down, visibly frustrated.*

**RYAN:** Okay. That other lender was not what the mailer said.

**JORDAN:** What happened?

**RYAN:** Rate crept up once they actually pulled everything. And the "extra closing cost help" turned into basically nothing. So — I'm back.

**JORDAN:** Okay. Good, we can pick right back up. Did you find a house?

**RYAN:** Actually — that place on Willow we looked at before this whole detour. I want to put in an offer $25,000 under list, plus ask for $10,000 toward closing costs, and still use the down payment assistance so I'm not bringing anything out of pocket.

**JORDAN:** Ryan — that listing had multiple showings the day it went up. An offer that low, asking for that much back, with zero out of pocket — that's not going to read as competitive.

**RYAN:** I just don't want to overextend.

**JORDAN:** I get that, but there's a difference between a smart offer and a losing one. Let me pull it up so we can see where it actually stands right now.

*Jordan turns to her screen. Pause.*

**JORDAN:** Ryan... it's already under contract. It went two days after it listed.

**RYAN:** What? It's been two weeks!

**JORDAN:** I know. Between the flyer detour and figuring out this offer, that's exactly how much time passed.

**RYAN:** My wife loved that house.

**JORDAN:** I know she did. This is exactly why speed and a realistic offer both matter — losing either one can cost you the house you actually wanted.
