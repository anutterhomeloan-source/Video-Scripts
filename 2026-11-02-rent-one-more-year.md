## Hook

I'm a loan officer.  
And for some of you… I think you should rent one more year.  
Yeah. I said it.  
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.  
And sometimes that means telling you "not yet."  

## Problem

Right now, everybody's telling you to buy.  
Your coworker. Your mom. Your landlord, raising the rent again.  
"You're just throwing money away."  

> VISUAL: text overlay "Renting = throwing money away?"

## Empathy

And I get it. That pressure is real.  
Nobody wants to feel like they're falling behind.  

## Answer + Stance

But here's my take.  
The right year beats the fast year.  
Buying before you're ready doesn't put you ahead.  
It just gets you a mortgage you stress about every month.  
So here's how to tell which one you are.  
Three questions. Be honest.  
One. After your down payment and closing costs… would you still have savings left?  
Not zero. Actual savings.  
Two. Is your income steady?  
Like, would you bet on it for the next twelve months?  
Three. Are you planning to stay in this area at least three years?  

> VISUAL: checklist overlay, three boxes checking in as you say each one

## Change

If you said yes to all three? You're probably more ready than you think. And we should talk.  
If you said no to one of them… that's not a "never."  
That's a plan.  
Use the next twelve months to fix that one thing.  
Build the cushion. Get the job history. Figure out where you actually want to live.  

## End Result + CTA

Then next year, you're not the scared buyer hoping it works out.  
You're the buyer who walks in ready.  
And honestly? Those are my favorite clients.  
So be honest with me.  
Comment "this year" or "next year."  
I want to see where everybody's at.
