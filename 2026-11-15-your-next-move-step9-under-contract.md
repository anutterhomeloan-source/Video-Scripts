## Script

The seller said yes. You're under contract.

And this is the part where most people relax, and it's actually when the clock starts.

This is step nine of nine in Your Next Move.

VISUAL: Your Next Move board graphic, Step 9 (Under Contract!) highlighted — your-next-move-step9.png

So here's what I want you to do in the first few days.

First, your earnest money. Get it sent on time. And before you wire anything, call the title company at a number you already know is real, not one from an email. Wire fraud is real, and it happens right at this step.

Second, schedule your inspection right away. You only have so many days in your due diligence period, and they go fast.

Third, send your contract to your loan officer the same day you sign it, so we can get your appraisal ordered and keep everything moving.

VISUAL: Checklist overlay: EARNEST MONEY (verify by phone) / INSPECTION / CONTRACT TO YOUR LO

And fourth, the big one. Don't change anything. Don't open a new credit card, don't finance furniture, don't switch jobs, and don't move big chunks of money around without asking me first.

Because being under contract isn't the finish line. It's the start of the part where everything you've already done has to hold up.

So that's all nine steps, everything nobody tells you before you're under contract.

And if you're somewhere on this list and you don't know where to start, start with me. The link is in my bio, and we'll figure out your next move together.
