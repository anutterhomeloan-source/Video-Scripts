## Hook
You paid it off in 4 easy installments.
No interest. Not even on your credit report.
Underwriting still counts it against you.

## Branding
If you don't know who I am, my name is Jordan Nutter, and I help make mortgages make sense.

## Problem
Buy-now-pay-later feels harmless. Split it into 4 payments, no big deal, right?

## Empathy
It's marketed that way on purpose. Nobody tells you it matters for a mortgage.

## Answer
Here's the thing — even when Klarna, Afterpay, or Affirm doesn't show up on your credit report, it shows up on your bank statements.
And underwriters look at your bank statements.
Those payments get counted in your debt-to-income ratio, same as a credit card.

VISUAL: Text overlay — "Klarna / Afterpay / Affirm = counted in your DTI" (klarna-dti-card.png)

## Change
So once you're preapproved, treat BNPL exactly like a new credit card.
Pause it. Don't open new ones. Don't stack multiple at once.

VISUAL: Checklist overlay — "Preapproved? Pause: credit cards / BNPL / new loans"

## End Result
One less invisible thing that can quietly blow up your closing.
