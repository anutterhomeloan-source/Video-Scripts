## Hook
I'm a loan officer. I still got my own closing costs wrong.

## Branding
If you don't know me — I'm Jordan, and I walk buyers through their closing costs for a living. I still messed up my own.

## Problem
When I bought my own house, I thought I had it handled. I estimated my closing costs. I felt good about the number. Then I got my closing disclosure.

## Empathy
Turns out, my house was in an HOA. And nobody tells you about the HOA transfer fee. Or the prorated dues you owe the second you close.

VISUAL: mock closing disclosure excerpt, "HOA Transfer Fee" and "Prorated HOA Dues" circled (closing-cost-mistake-card.png)

## Answer
Those aren't "extra." They're part of your closing costs. I just didn't budget for them. And it wasn't just that — there were a couple other small things I underestimated too. Little line items that add up fast.

## Change
Ask for a full, itemized closing cost estimate. Not just a round number like "3% of the purchase price." If you're buying into an HOA, ask specifically: is there a transfer fee? Are there prorated dues due at closing?

VISUAL: text overlay — "Ask about: HOA transfer fee, prorated dues, prepaid interest"

## End Result
I do this for a living, and it still caught me off guard. So don't assume you've got it all mapped out just because you did the math once.
