## Script

Most buyers think negotiating means one thing, which is the price.

But some of the best wins I've seen for my clients had nothing to do with the price.

This is step eight of nine in Your Next Move.

VISUAL: Your Next Move board graphic, Step 8 (Negotiate) highlighted — your-next-move-step8.png

So here's what a lot of people don't realize. You can ask the seller for more than just a lower number.

You can ask them to help with your closing costs. That's money you don't have to bring to the table.

You can ask for money toward buying your rate down, which can lower your monthly payment for the whole loan.

VISUAL: Text call-outs: "CLOSING COSTS" · "RATE BUYDOWN" · "REPAIRS" · "CLOSING DATE"

And after your inspection, you can ask for repairs, or for a credit instead so you can handle them yourself.

And sometimes it's the closing date. A seller who needs to move fast might give a little on something else if you're flexible on timing.

And honestly, right now, a lot of sellers are open to it. Homes are sitting longer, and plenty of sellers would rather help with your costs than drop the price again.

So here's my advice. Before your agent writes the offer, talk to your loan officer about what would actually help you most. Sometimes a few thousand toward your rate does more for you than a few thousand off the price.

You never get what you don't ask for.

That's step eight. Step nine is the big one. The seller says yes, you're under contract, and I'll walk you through what happens in the first few days.
