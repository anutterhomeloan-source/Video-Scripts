## Hook

Nobody's buying houses right now.  
…And that's exactly why this might be the most leverage buyers have had in years.  
I'm Jordan Nutter. I'm a loan officer here in Atlanta, and I help make mortgages make sense.  
And I'm watching this happen in real time.  

## Receipts

Here's what's going on.  
Across the 50 biggest metros, sellers now outnumber buyers by about 63%.  
The number of buyers is the lowest Redfin has on record going back to 2013.  
And pending home sales just hit the lowest level ever recorded.  
VISUAL: Bar: "Sellers 163 / Buyers 100" (Redfin via AP, Oct 25) + HousingWire "record low" headline
Here in Atlanta? Homes for sale just hit a more-than-decade high.  
And pending contracts are down almost 25% from last year.  
VISUAL: Hoodline / Atlanta Agent Magazine screenshot (Oct 15): pending -24.8%

## What People Have Wrong

Most people see headlines like that and think, "Bad market. Stay away."  
But think about it from the seller's side.  
When there are more sellers than buyers…  
the seller needs you more than you need them.  

## We Were Just Here

And we were literally here in 2023.  
Rates hit almost 8%. Everyone froze.  
And the buyers who kept going?  
A lot of them walked away with seller credits and rate buydowns.  

## Agency

So if you're buying right now, don't just ask for a lower price.  
Ask the seller to cover closing costs.  
Ask for money to buy down your rate.  
Ask for the repairs.  
You're allowed to ask. Right now, a lot of sellers are saying yes.  

## Stance

Here's my take.  
Fewer buyers is your leverage. Use it.  

## Close + CTA

When everyone runs from the market, the prepared buyer gets to set the terms.  
So I want to know.  
Would you buy in a market nobody else is buying in? Yes or no.
