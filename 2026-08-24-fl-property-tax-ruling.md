## Problem
The ballot title says "Save Our Homes From Excessive Property Taxes." Sounds like elimination. Sounds like relief.

## Empathy
And I get why that headline grabs you — property taxes are one of the biggest hidden costs in your payment.

## Answer
Here's what the title doesn't say.
This isn't elimination. It raises the homestead exemption — from $50,000 now, to $150,000 in 2027, to $250,000 in 2028.
And a judge just ruled the ballot language itself is misleading — full of "political taglines" instead of a fair explanation. It has to be rewritten before it goes in front of voters.

VISUAL: Comparison overlay — "Ballot title: 'Save Our Homes'" / "What it does: exemption $50K → $250K by 2028"

## Change
So here's what I want you to actually take from this — property taxes live inside your escrow, and they can change your payment even when your rate never moves.
An exemption increase like this would lower that part of your payment. It doesn't erase it.

## Stance
Should you wait on this ballot measure to fix your budget? No — not even the people writing it can agree on how to describe it yet.

## End Result
Know what's actually in your escrow, so a campaign slogan doesn't set your expectations for you.
